# Technical Decision Records

Record a decision here when it is consequential, difficult to reverse, likely to be questioned later, or affects multiple teammates. Routine implementation choices do not need a record.

Use filenames such as `0001-dataset-storage-policy.md`. Assign the next number when the decision is created.

## Template

```markdown
# NNNN: Decision title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners:
- Related work:

## Context

What problem, constraints, and evidence led to this decision?

## Decision

What did the team decide?

## Alternatives considered

What credible alternatives were considered and why were they not selected?

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, required, or intentionally deferred?

## Validation or review trigger

How will the team know the decision works, and what evidence should cause it to be revisited?
```

Do not rewrite an accepted record to hide its history. Create a new record that supersedes it.
