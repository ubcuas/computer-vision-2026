## Updating this register

For every new or revised source:

1. Preserve the unmodified source under `sources/` with its version or date in the filename.
2. Record its provenance and SHA-256 hash.
3. Mark whether it is current, supplemental, or superseded.
4. Review `task-1.md`, `ambiguities.md`, and affected technical decisions.
5. Preserve superseded sources for traceability rather than overwriting them.
